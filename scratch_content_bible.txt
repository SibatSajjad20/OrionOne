How to Use This Document

This is the authoritative content source for the Orion One website. Every headline, line of body copy, CTA, and piece of microcopy that appears on the live site should originate here. Where design or development needs to deviate (character limits, technical constraints), flag it back to Reamarc rather than rewriting in place — tone consistency is the entire value of this document.

Tone of voice — the standard every chapter is held to:

Not this:

Luxury apartments overlooking the lake.

This:

Every morning begins with water, light, and an uninterrupted horizon. Every evening ends where the city slows and the shoreline comes alive.

Words we do not use: luxury apartments, book now, best investment, premium living, world-class (unless earned by a specific fact), state-of-the-art, dream home, once-in-a-lifetime.

What we write instead: specific, sensory, restrained. We describe a moment, not a feature. We let the reader arrive at "luxury" themselves — we never say the word to earn it.

Reference register: Aman's stillness, Apple's confidence-through-simplicity, Porsche's precision, Bentley Residences' quiet authority, Foster + Partners' architectural intelligence, Four Seasons Private Residences' warmth without excess.

01.1 — Hero

Supporting headline (appears after headline settles, smaller, beneath):

Microcopy — scroll cue (bottom of viewport, small, understated):

Image/video direction: Fullscreen drone footage or a slow, high-resolution image sequence. Open on mist over still water at first light — no building visible yet. Over 3–5 seconds, the camera drifts forward and the tower's silhouette begins to resolve out of the haze, backlit by early sun. Palette: cool blues and greys warming to gold. No people in this shot — the emptiness is intentional; it lets the viewer project themselves into the space rather than watch someone else occupy it.

Motion notes: Headline fades in at 0.6s, 500ms duration, slight upward drift (12–16px) as it appears — no bounce, no overshoot. Supporting headline follows at 1.4s. Background media on a slow, continuous Ken Burns drift (barely perceptible — the eye should feel calm, not notice movement happening). Navigation chrome (logo, menu icon) fades in last, at ~2.2s, once the emotional beat has landed — the visitor should not see UI before they see the lake.

02.3 — Philosophy

Section headline:

Architecture That Behaves Like Water

Body copy:

Orion One's design philosophy starts from a simple observation: water doesn't have corners. Its towers curve rather than turn, its terraces step back in rhythm rather than stack in repetition, and its facade reads less like a wall and more like a shoreline — different from every angle, softened by planting at every level.

The result is a building that doesn't fight its site. It extends it.

Storytelling section — "Three Principles":

Principle 01 — Fluidity

Every curved balcony line traces back to the movement of water — the architects' way of keeping the lake present even on the building's highest floors.

Principle 02 — Light

The organic massing was shaped specifically to maximize natural light and unobstructed views, rather than maximizing floor plate efficiency at the expense of both.

Principle 03 — Connection

Landscaped terraces at multiple levels blur the line between interior and exterior — nature is not something residents visit, but something the building is woven through.

Microcopy — numbered labels (for the three-principle layout):

01 / 02 / 03

Image/video direction: Close architectural detail shots — the terrace planting, the curved balcony geometry, a facade detail catching light — treated almost like product photography. This is the chapter's most "designed object" visual moment, in contrast to Chapter 01's lifestyle imagery.

Motion notes: Three-principle section reveals left-to-right in sequence (staggered 150ms apart) rather than all at once — reinforces "three distinct ideas," not one paragraph.

02.5 — Masterplan

Section headline:

One Address, Three Ways to Belong to It

Body copy:

Orion One's masterplan organizes around a simple hierarchy: residences above, commerce and lifestyle at grade, and the lakefront promenade stitching every level back to the water. Nothing on the site is purely circulation — even the paths between buildings were designed as places to be, not just ways to get through.

Storytelling sections (masterplan zones):

Residential Levels (3rd–9th floor)

One and two-bedroom residences, elevated above the activity of the commercial floors, oriented toward the lake or boulevard.

Ground & First Floor — Commercial

Restaurants, cafés, and boutique retail opening directly onto covered and open-air lakefront dining terraces.

The Promenade

A continuous lakeside walking and gathering path connecting Orion One to DHA Phase III's wider waterfront district — the community club, dancing fountains, and hotel precinct.

Image/video direction: A clean, top-down masterplan diagram (architectural line-drawing style, not a photorealistic render) — zone-labeled, animated to build up layer by layer as described in the copy: base site → promenade → commercial → residential.

Motion notes: This is a strong candidate for the "interactive masterplan" WebGL/SVG moment referenced in the technical strategy — zones highlight on hover/scroll-sync with the copy above.
